# T07 Graphics devices

## Checked

One plot with non-ASCII text (€, é, α, ≤, CJK, •, ≥, µ) and plot2's own labels on `pdf()`, `cairo_pdf()`, `png()` (cairo and default), `svg()` and `ragg::agg_png()`:

- `cairo_pdf`, `png`, `svg`, `agg_png`: no warnings.
- `pdf()` (default for `ggsave("*.pdf")` and R Markdown PDF output): 11 encoding warnings; characters replaced by dots. Two of them are generated by plot2 itself: the bullet in scientific labels (`cli::symbol$bullet`) and the euro sign of `euros()` (F107, new). This explains the `mbcsToSbcs` warning in the `?plot2` examples seen in R05 (secondary axis example).
- With `options(cli.unicode = FALSE)` scientific labels change to `3 * 10^8` (F107).

## Findings

F107 (new); F011 (showtext changes rendering on every device in the session).
